# Animated story template v1. Copy to cartoon\episodes\<name>\screenplay.txt, fill it in, run: make_cartoon.py <name> all
#
# FORMAT: a first-person "this happened to me" story told by a recurring narrator (NARRATOR: <cast id>), acted out by the
# cast in cartoon scenes. Recurring characters with their own lives (cast.json "bio") are what bring viewers back.
#
# PACKAGING FIRST
#  - Title: first person, concrete, a little alarming, under 60 characters ("My Perfect Neighbor Had a Key to My Apartment").
#  - 2-3 THUMB lines (YouTube Test & Compare): 1-3 huge words, one big scared/shocked face, the antagonist smiling behind.
#
# STRUCTURE (8-10 min = 1,250-1,500 words, about 150 spoken words per minute plus pauses; check the TTS total)
#  0:00-0:40  COLD OPEN: start at the most alarming moment (the middle of the story), show it, then "My name is ... and this
#             is how ..." to camera ({cam}). Then -- title.
#  0:40-2:00  WHO WE ARE: the place, the narrator's stakes (what they can lose and why it matters to them), the cast in
#             one line each, with one human detail.
#  2:00-4:00  SOMETHING DOESN'T FIT: three strange details, numbered out loud ("Strange thing number one..."), each in its own scene.
#  ~4:00      BACK TO THE COLD OPEN: "Which brings us back to..." and the first real discovery. Raise the stakes.
#  4:00-6:00  INVESTIGATE: the narrator takes a risk; a friend helps badly (comedy); a reveal shown with an {insert: ...}.
#  6:00-7:30  CONFRONTATION: the antagonist attacks in public; the narrator wins with something set up earlier (a callback).
#  7:30-8:30  TWIST + HOOK: a quiet scene that reframes the story in one line, then a new threat for the next episode.
#  end        -- card 3.5 {pop: TO BE CONTINUED...}   then   -- card 10 {pop: <A QUESTION FOR THE COMMENTS>}
#
# CRAFT RULES
#  - Every line under 18 words. Narration says what we can't see; dialogue does the rest. A joke every ~30 s.
#  - Direct almost every line: [name: pose expression]. Expressions change faces; held poses (props) persist, gestures last one line.
#  - Use {insert: name} pictures for every clue (a key, a letter, a wall of photos): they are what viewers remember.
#  - {zoom} on reveals, {hold: 0.8} after the biggest ones, {shake} on shocks.
#  - Reuse locations from earlier episodes (cartoon\locations\) so the series has a home; new ones cost ~30 s each.
#  - New poses and expressions cost ~1 min each the first time per character, then they are free forever.

TITLE: <First-person title>
NARRATOR: nia
